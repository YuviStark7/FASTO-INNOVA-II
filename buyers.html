A farmer near you may write to you through Fasto Innova

Fasto Innova helps small farms around Cassino reach nearby restaurants, hotels, shops and markets directly. This page explains what it is, why your business is on our list, and what you can do about it.

How it works

  1. The farmer talks to the AI

    A farmer describes their farm in their own words: what they grow, roughly how much a week, which months, and how far they are from Cassino.

  2. The AI looks for buyers nearby

    It compares that farm against the list below and ranks the closest fits by product, quantity and distance. Then it drafts a short introduction — in Italian, with an English translation underneath.

  3. The farmer decides whether to send it

    Nothing is sent automatically. The farmer reads the draft, changes it if they want, and sends it themselves. If you are interested, a logistics partner arranges the pickup and the delivery — that hand-off is the point of the project, not an introduction and a shrug.

What this means for you

  • You do not need an account, an app or a password. There is nothing to install and nothing to pay.
  • A message you receive was written for your business, but an AI composed it from the farmer's answers. Treat the quantities and the months as that farmer's offer, and confirm them with them directly.
  • No price is agreed here. Any figure this project puts on an order is a desk-research assumption used to size it — never a price a farmer or a buyer has accepted.
  • Being on the list is not an agreement. It does not mean your business has been contacted, has replied, or has committed to anything.

Why your business is on this list

The list was built by desk research in July 2026 from public sources — review platforms, business directories, official websites and local press. Nobody was visited, phoned or written to in order to build it, and every entry names the public source it came from.

What is held about your business is what those listings already say in public: the name, the kind of business, the area, and roughly how far it is from Cassino.

There is also a guess — which products you would plausibly buy, and roughly what volume. It was inferred from the kind of food you serve and from public reviews, to give the prototype something to rank against. It is not a statement about your business, which is why it is deliberately not published on this page. Ask, and you will be told exactly what is written next to your name.

Ask for a correction, or to be removed from the list altogether, and it will be done. No reason needed.

What this is, honestly

Fasto Innova is a student prototype built during an internship at R&S Management. It is not a company, it takes no commission and it handles nobody's money. Matching runs against a hand-curated list rather than against buyers who signed up — which is exactly why this page exists.
