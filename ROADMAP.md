# Fasto Innova — Improvement Roadmap

How this file works: items live in **Approved (queue order)** until they ship, then move to **Done** with a date and a short changelog note. The daily automation always works the top not-yet-done item in the queue and never adds new unapproved scope on its own — new ideas go through **Proposed** and need a yes from Yuvi first.

Size key: **S** = a few hours of equivalent work, frontend-only, low risk · **M** = half a day equivalent, may touch the database · **L** = a real feature, bigger than one day, not meant for the daily queue.
